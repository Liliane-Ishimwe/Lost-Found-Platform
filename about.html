Overview of a Lost and Found Platform

A Lost and Found Platform is an online system either a website ,mobile app,or both created to help individuals and organizations manage lost or found items.The goal is to simplify the process of locating and recovering lost belongings by providing a shared space where users can report and search for items in real-time.

Who uses the Platform

1.Individuals:

People who lose or find personal belongings in public or private spaces(e.g:Phones,keys,wallets,ID,school items).

2.Organizations:

Schools,airports,hotels,shopping malls,and public transport systems use such platforms to manage lost and found items systematically

Benefits of the Platform

1.Increases the chances of item recovery.

2. Improves public trust and responsibility.

3. Reduces waste from unclaimed found items.

4.Provides transparency and organization in places like schools,malls,or transport services.

Purpose of the Platform

1.Reconnect lost items with their rightful owners.

2.Promote honesty and community cooperation.

3.Save time and reduce stress for people who've lost valuable belongings.

4.Reduce the number of unclaimed or discarded found items.
