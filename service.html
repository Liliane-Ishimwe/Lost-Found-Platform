Found Item Reporting

Users who find misplaced items can post details such as the item's appearance,where it was found,and the time/date ,allowing the rightful owner to claim it.

Lost Item Reporting

Users can submit detailed reports about items they have lost,including the item's description,where and when it was, and photos to help identify it.

  • The platform may assist in verifying ownership by requiring proof(e.g:serial number,unique details,or identification)before allowing a claim

  • Once a potential match is found ,the platform allows the owner and the finder to communicate securely to verify ownership and arrange a return.

  • The system can notify users when a new item matching their lost/found report is posted , helping speed up the recovery process.
