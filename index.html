Lost&Found Platform

Is designed to help people report and recover lost or found items.It acts as a central place where users can either.Report a lost item by describing it and when/where it was lost.Report a found item by uploading details and trying to connect it with the rightful owner.Reunite people with their lost belongings and promote honesty and community support.

Users can submit detailed reports about items they have lost,including the item's description,where and when it was, and photos to help identify it.

Users who find misplaced items can post details such as the item's appearance,where it was found,and the time/date ,allowing the rightful owner to claim it.
