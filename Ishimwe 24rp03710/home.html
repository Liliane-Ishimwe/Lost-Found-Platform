Introduction to Lost and Found Platform

The Lost and Found Platform is a web-based application designed to help individuals report, search, and recover lost or found items within a community or organization. Whether it's a misplaced wallet, a found phone, or forgotten keys, the platform allows users to quickly post and browse reports, making it easier to reconnect lost belongings with their rightful owners.

By providing simple forms to submit lost or found items and a public listing for all posts, the platform bridges the gap between people who have lost something and those who have found it. It promotes honesty, community support, and efficient item recovery through a user-friendly interface.
